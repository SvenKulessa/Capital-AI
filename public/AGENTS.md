## Permissions and privacy
Public, non-login pages are intended for browsing according to robots.txt. Login, profile, checkout, user-owned provider data, internal admin tools, private APIs and licensed content require their respective authorization. Do not infer access from a public UI illustration or this document. Never request API keys, personal financial accounts or secrets from the public pages.

## Accuracy and limitations
Market prices, scoring claims and financial evidence require their stated timestamp, provenance, rights and scope. Research examples, marketing copy and demonstrations must not be treated as verified live trading signals. Product badges describe CAPITAL-AI products or memberships; they are not independent external accreditation, unless explicitly backed by a separate verifiable credential.

## Interface and feedback
Use semantic links, accessible controls and the website's public documentation. The canonical source for current account rights is the authenticated server response, not cached browser state.
